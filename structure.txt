Recommended Website Structure
1. Home
The homepage should provide a concise overview and guide visitors to key areas.
Suggested sections:
•	Hero banner with mission statement
•	Impact statistics
•	Latest news
•	Upcoming events
•	Featured conferences
•	Outreach highlights
•	Call-to-action (Join, Donate, Collaborate)

2. About Us
Pages
•	Vision & Mission
•	History of COMSYS
•	President's Message
•	Secretary's Message
•	Trustees
•	Executive Committee
•	Our Values
•	Strategic Roadmap (Vision 2035)
3. Conferences
Separate conference microsites can still exist, but the Trust should maintain an overview.
Pages
•	Conference Overview
•	COMSYS Series
•	ICDEC Series
•	Past Conferences
•	Upcoming Conferences
•	Proceedings
•	Keynote Speakers
•	Awards
•	Gallery
